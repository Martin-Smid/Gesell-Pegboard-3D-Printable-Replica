# Gesell Pegboard – 3D Printable Replica

A 3D printable replica of the pegboard used in the Gesell Developmental Schedules.
The board has four holes (two round, two square, in two sizes) with matching pegs.

> **Note:** This is an unofficial replica, not original Gesell test material.
> Results obtained with it may not be directly comparable to the standardized kit.

## Files

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `peg_board_30cm.stl` | Board, 300 mm (original size) |
| `peg_board_28cm.stl` | Board, 280 mm (for smaller print beds) |
| `peg_board_28cm.step` | CAD source for editing |
| `smaller_circ_peg5.4mm.stl` | Round peg for the Ø 6.4 mm hole |
| `bigger_circ_peg8.5mm.stl` | Round peg for the Ø 9.5 mm hole |
| `smaller_square_peg4.8mm.stl` | Square peg for the 6.4 mm hole |
| `bigger_square_peg7.9mm.stl` | Square peg for the 9.5 mm hole |

## Dimensions

- **Hole spacing is always 60 mm** center to center, on both boards.
- **30 cm board** follows the original: 60 mm from the edge to the first hole center.
- **28 cm board** is identical except the edge margins are 50 mm instead of 60 mm.
  It exists only for convenience, so it fits on printers with smaller beds.
- Board: 280 / 300 × 76.5 × 19 mm, holes 17 mm deep.
- Pegs: 70 mm long.

| Hole | Peg | Clearance per side |
|---|---|---|
| Round Ø 6.4 mm | Ø 5.4 mm | 0.5 mm |
| Round Ø 9.5 mm | Ø 8.5 mm | 0.5 mm |
| Square 6.4 mm | 4.8 mm | 0.8 mm |
| Square 9.5 mm | 7.9 mm | 0.8 mm |

Clearances are intentionally generous, since holes usually print smaller on FDM printers.
If your printer is accurate, you can enlarge the pegs in the STEP file.

## Printing tips

- Use a brim on the board; long flat parts tend to warp.
- Print pegs with 4+ walls or 100 % infill so they don't snap. PETG is tougher than PLA.
- If pegs still don't fit, sand them, drill out the round holes, or use
  *Horizontal Expansion* of about −0.1 mm in your slicer.

## Safety

Use only under adult supervision. Discard broken or damaged pegs.
